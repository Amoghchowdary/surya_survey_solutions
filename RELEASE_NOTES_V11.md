# Surya Survey Solutions V11

## Scope
Full responsive QA pass across the entire website while preserving the approved desktop design.

## Responsive coverage
- Phone: 320–767px
- Tablet: 768–1199px
- Desktop: 1200px+

## Corrected
- Header/navbar and mobile menu containment
- Homepage hero, CTA buttons, trust cards and all homepage sections
- Shared internal page hero
- About page grids, mission/vision cards and equipment sections
- Projects filters, project cards, dialog, metadata and gallery
- Contact information, quick actions, form fields and sticky phone actions
- Statistics, client-logo marquee, CTA band and footer
- Horizontal overflow, image scaling, card widths and long text wrapping
- Tiny-phone and landscape/short-height safeguards

Desktop styles at 1200px+ are intentionally preserved: the responsive QA overrides are scoped below 1200px, and the original desktop utility classes remain in place.
