# V35 — Timed Website Launch Gate

- Adds a full-site launch countdown to 19 November 2026 at 11:00 AM IST.
- Before launch, only the countdown experience is rendered.
- At zero, a single short radial burst/particle launch animation runs.
- After the animation, the complete existing website opens automatically.
- Visits after 11:00 AM IST bypass the countdown and animation and open the website directly.
- No manual enter button is required.
- V34 equipment carousels and all prior production functionality are preserved.
